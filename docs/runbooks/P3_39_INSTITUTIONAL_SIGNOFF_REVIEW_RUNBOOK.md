## Failure handling

If institutional signoff review evidence is incomplete, stop, keep institutional signoff review status blocked, record missing evidence, record affected client, record responsible owner, and do not start readiness status transition review.

## P3.39 conclusion

Institutional signoff review must be completed before readiness status transition review is considered.
