Project context:

Cáritas Brigadas de Salud is a health brigade operations platform intended to support patient registration, brigade operations, clinical service encounters, auditability, offline-first workflows, and institutional reporting.

The system is not just a mobile app. It is a modular institutional platform with backend, database, operational evidence, security guardrails, documentation, and future mobile/frontend integration.
